The work happens once. The speed stays.

Your first request takes the time it should. Each request after that builds on what we already know about your business.

The first time

  1. Tell us about the company

    Registration, GSTIN, income-tax filings and the people who run the business.

  2. Show how the business runs

    Recent bank statements, receivables and confirmed work orders.

  3. Get a clear decision

    Your approved limit, the repayment schedule and the reasons behind both.

Every time after

  1. Request again in minutes

    Keep your statements current and send a request from your phone. We check what changed and decide.

Software reads the paperwork. Our credit team makes the call.

Our underwriting platform does the slow parts of lending in minutes, so the people deciding on your loan spend their time on your business, not on spreadsheets.

  • Bank statements read line by line

    Monthly inflows, cheque returns, low-balance days and customer concentration, from every account you share.

  • Tax and GST compliance checked

    Filing regularity and turnover are matched against what your bank statements show.

  • Your limit reviewed every month

    As your cash flow grows, your limit can grow with it, without a fresh application.

  • Every decision explained

    You see what supports your limit, what holds it back and what would raise it.
